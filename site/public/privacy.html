Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version: we collect what you type into the waitlist form, and nothing about your browsing. There are no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and every file this site uses comes from this site.

Who we are

Opalix is run by [COMPANY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. We decide how the data described here is used, which makes us its controller under the GDPR.

For anything about your data, write to privacy@opalix.ai.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist

  • Your email address. So we can tell you when your spot opens.
  • The plan you chose. Individual or Team.
  • Your role, if you picked one. It's optional.
  • Which button brought you to the form, for example the one in the pricing section.
  • Your country, as our host works it out from your connection. We store the country only, never your IP address.
  • When you signed up, and when you last changed your details.

When you browse the site

Nothing is stored about you. We don't set cookies, and we don't run analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts, styles and images are served by this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else.

What our host sees

Cloudflare delivers this site. Like any host, it handles your IP address to send you the pages and to stop abuse. We use it to limit each address to five waitlist signups a minute. We don't store those addresses, and we don't use them to identify you.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • Emailing you about access relies on your consent, which you give by submitting the form (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Plan, role, source and country help us decide which labs and plans to open first. This relies on our legitimate interest in understanding demand (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Blocking spam and abuse also relies on our legitimate interest, in keeping the waitlist real and the site available (Article 6(1)(f)).

We only email you about Opalix access. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

Who processes it for us

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site and the database the waitlist is stored in, acting as our processor under its data processing terms. Cloudflare may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where it does, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

If we add another provider, for example to send emails, we'll list it here before we use it.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist details until you ask us to remove you. If you never ask, we delete them 12 months after paid plans open, unless you've become a customer by then.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct it
  • delete it, including taking you off the waitlist
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use based on legitimate interest

Email privacy@opalix.ai from the address you signed up with. We'll reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Cookies

This site doesn't use any. If signing in or the labs ever need one, we'll list it here, and ask first where the law requires it.

What this page covers

This page covers the opalix.ai website and its waitlist. When accounts and the lab platform open to more people, we'll add what they collect here before they launch. If we change this page, we'll update the date at the top. If the change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll email you first.
