Only the pages that differ from video 2 (80 words); every later page is the same as there.

## Scene 1: Two hundred lines

1. **Hook: the interview question.** A classic platform interview question, and the answer has a name: an MCP gateway, this violet box. Ten agents times twenty servers is two hundred connections, each with its own secret, before the box arrives.
2. **One box, then zoom in.** Route every line through the box and ten plus twenty makes thirty, not two hundred. To see why, lift it out again and frame three agents, named Support, Billing and Ops, against five servers: weather, calculator, orders, docs, maps. Fifteen lines, redrawn one by one.
