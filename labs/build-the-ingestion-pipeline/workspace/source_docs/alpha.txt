Alpha runbook: rotating the on-call schedule.

Every Monday at 09:00 the on-call rotation hands off to the next engineer
on the roster. The handoff is not automatic -- the outgoing engineer must
post a summary of any open incidents in the team channel before 09:00,
and the incoming engineer must acknowledge it there before taking the
pager. If no acknowledgement lands within thirty minutes, the rotation
tool escalates to the secondary on-call automatically.

Incidents opened in the last twenty-four hours of a shift are the
outgoing engineer's responsibility to either close or hand off explicitly
by name; a rotation handoff never silently drops an open incident on the
floor. This has been the rule since the September postmortem, where an
incident sat untouched for six hours because neither engineer believed it
was theirs.
