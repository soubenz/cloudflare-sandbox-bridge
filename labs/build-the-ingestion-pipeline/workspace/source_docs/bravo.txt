Bravo runbook: OLD-BRAVO-MARKER-echo-relay message retries.

The echo-relay service retries a failed downstream publish up to three
times, with a fixed two-second delay between attempts, before it gives up
and writes the message to the dead-letter queue. Nothing currently pages
anyone when a message lands in the dead-letter queue -- it is checked by
hand once a day during business hours.

This is known to be too slow for anything time-sensitive, and is tracked
as a follow-up: the plan is to page on dead-letter depth once it crosses
a small threshold, rather than relying on a human noticing during the
daily check. OLD-BRAVO-MARKER-echo-relay is the identifier this runbook
uses for the service in every dashboard and alert that references it.
