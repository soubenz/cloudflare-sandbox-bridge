        How this console works
        
    
        Four things are worth knowing before you start a lab.
        
    
        
      
        
        
        Guide, terminal and editor
        
        
        The guide on the left says what you are asked to do: its Brief, the questions or checks, and the hints. The story and the lessons come before the lab starts. Hide it with the button above it when you want the room. On the right, work in the Terminal, or open a file from the tree in the Editor and save it with Ctrl S.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Checks
        
        
        Checks are graded and you can re-run them as often as you like. Each carries a weight, and your score is the weighted share that passes. A failing check says why.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Hints
        
        
        Hints unlock on a timer. The ones still to come are listed as locked, each with a countdown; there is no way to open one early.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Pressure
        
        
        Some labs change under you around a stated minute. The checks run against what is true at the end, not against what was true when you started.
        
      
        
    
        
    
        Timers. The header counts down to a hard limit, and a lab left alone ends after a warning. Use End & save to keep your work and pick it up again.

        When this lab ends, your workspace is cleared. Choose “End and keep my work” if you want to pick it up again later.
